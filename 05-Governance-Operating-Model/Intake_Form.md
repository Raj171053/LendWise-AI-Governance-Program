## 4. Data and Privacy

- What data will the system collect, receive, generate, or access?
- Does the data include personal, financial, sensitive, biometric, or confidential information?
- What is the source of the data?
- What is the proposed legal basis for personal-data processing, where applicable?
- What are the proposed retention and deletion arrangements?
- Will data be transferred across borders or processed by third parties?
- Are data quality, representativeness, access restrictions, and lineage documented?

## 6. Human Oversight and Explainability

- Will a human review the output before a consequential action?
- Can the reviewer understand the relevant information and limitations?
- Can the reviewer override or reject the output?
- Does the reviewer have sufficient authority, time, and training?
- How will overrides, disagreements, and escalations be recorded?
- What explanation will be available to affected users, where appropriate?

## 7. Testing and Monitoring

- What performance measures are proposed?
- What fairness and subgroup analyses are needed?
- What independent validation is required?
- What monitoring thresholds and incident triggers are proposed?
- How will material model, data, vendor, or purpose changes be detected?
- What evidence will be retained for audit and review?
