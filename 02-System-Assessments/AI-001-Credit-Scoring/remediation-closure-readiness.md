# AI-001 Remediation Closure Readiness

## Purpose

Assess whether each remediation item has sufficient evidence for an authorised closure decision.

## Closure Requirements

An item must not be closed solely because its documentation has been drafted, uploaded, or committed.

For each item, verify:

1. The finding and intended control outcome are clearly defined.
2. The remediation action has been implemented.
3. The required evidence exists and is traceable.
4. The control has been validated through an appropriate review or test.
5. Identified defects have been remediated and retested, where required.
6. An authorised person has reviewed and accepted the closure evidence.

## Human Oversight — Closure Evidence

Required evidence may include:

- Approved reviewer authority and escalation procedures.
- Confirmed ownership, backup routes, and approved service-level targets.
- Evidence that reviewers can access the information required to challenge AI scores.
- Evidence that escalation, override authorisation, fallback, and audit-trail controls function as intended.
- Actual test results for HOV-01 through HOV-05, with supporting evidence.
- Defect records, remediation evidence, and retest outcomes where applicable.
- Documented closure approval by the authorised owner.

## Status Definitions

- **Open:** The finding remains unresolved.
- **In progress:** Remediation is underway.
- **Ready for validation:** Implementation evidence is available and awaiting independent or designated validation.
- **Blocked:** A dependency prevents completion.
- **Closed:** Required evidence has been reviewed and accepted by the authorised closure owner.
