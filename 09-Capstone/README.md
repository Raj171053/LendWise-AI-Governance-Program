# LendWise AI Governance Program — Capstone

## Overview

This capstone consolidates the AI governance portfolio developed for the fictional LendWise Financial Services Ltd. It focuses on AI-001 Credit Scoring and its governance, model-risk, evidence, and regulatory-readiness limitations.

## Capstone Deliverables

1. **Mock Audit** — `Mock_Audit.md` and `Mock_Audit.pdf` if a PDF version is maintained. Documents the simulated audit scope, findings, evidence limitations, and remediation conditions.
2. **Board Presentation** — `Board_Presentation.md` and `Board_Presentation.pdf`. Summarizes principal risks, governance work completed, remediation priorities, and the proposed board decision.
3. **Executive Summary** — `Executive_Summary.md` and `Executive_Summary.pdf`. Provides a concise account of the current governance position and recommended decision.

## Final Governance Position

**AI-001 Credit Scoring: NOT APPROVED — REMEDIATION REQUIRED.**

The portfolio documents identified risks and proposed controls. It does not demonstrate that all controls have been implemented, independently tested, or shown to operate effectively.

## Important Limitations

- LendWise is a fictional case study.
- The model audit uses public educational data, not production customer data.
- The mock audit is a simulation, not an independent audit opinion.
- Regulatory applicability and framework mappings require appropriate legal and compliance validation.
- Portfolio completion does not establish regulatory compliance, certification, or production readiness.

## Intended Use

This portfolio demonstrates a structured approach to AI governance assessment, risk documentation, control design, evidence traceability, and executive reporting. It should be reviewed as an educational and professional portfolio exercise.
