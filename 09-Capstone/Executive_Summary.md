# LendWise AI Governance Program — Executive Summary

**System:** AI-001 Credit Scoring  
**Organization:** LendWise Financial Services Ltd.  
**Markets considered:** European Union and India  
**Document status:** Portfolio simulation — Draft  
**Decision:** NOT APPROVED — REMEDIATION REQUIRED

## 1. Purpose

This executive summary presents the current governance position for LendWise's fictional AI-001 Credit Scoring System. It consolidates the portfolio's risk assessments, model audit, governance design, conformity-readiness work, and remediation findings.

## 2. Current Position

The governance program has produced a structured set of artifacts covering AI inventory, risk management, impact assessment, bias and explainability, governance accountability, AI agent controls, high-risk conformity readiness, and cross-framework mapping.

These materials establish a documented approach for identifying risks and planning controls. They do not establish that every proposed control is implemented, independently tested, or operating effectively.

## 3. Principal Outstanding Risks

The consolidated AI-001 tracker records 11 findings, F-001 through F-011. The principal areas requiring attention include:

- Proxy-variable and fairness risks.
- Label reliability and data representativeness.
- Human oversight and restrictions on automated adverse decisions.
- Monitoring, incident response, and change-triggered reassessment.
- Operator competence, stakeholder feedback, and risk communication.
- Independent finding closure and residual-risk approval.

## 4. Model Audit Limitations

The educational model audit used the public UCI German Credit dataset and a logistic regression baseline. The recorded results and subgroup analysis illustrate the need for more than aggregate accuracy when evaluating a credit-risk model.

These results are educational and cannot be treated as performance or fairness evidence for a deployed LendWise model.

## 5. Regulatory Readiness

The portfolio includes a preliminary EU AI Act gap assessment, a technical documentation skeleton, and a cross-framework mapping that references NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and relevant Indian data protection and AI governance considerations.

Legal applicability, organizational roles, applicable implementation dates, and conformity requirements require validation. The portfolio does not claim legal compliance, certification, or completed conformity assessment.

## 6. Recommended Decision

Maintain the production hold on AI-001.

Before reconsidering approval, management should require:

1. Remediation or documented resolution of material risk findings.
2. Verified implementation and appropriate testing of required controls.
3. Independent review of material model and control evidence.
4. Complete and accurate system-specific technical documentation.
5. A documented legal applicability assessment and completion of applicable regulatory steps.
6. Evidence-based closure decisions and authorized residual-risk decisions.

## 7. Conclusion

LendWise has established a structured AI governance portfolio and identified material areas requiring further assurance. The next priority is to move from documented design to verified implementation, effective testing, and independent review.

**Final position: AI-001 remains NOT APPROVED — REMEDIATION REQUIRED.**

This is a fictional portfolio exercise, not an independent audit opinion, regulatory certification, or legal determination.
